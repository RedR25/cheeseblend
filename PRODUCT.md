# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Hackathon judges.** A few minutes with the live demo. They're judging how well it uses Meta Muse, how deep the research goes, and how polished it feels. They need the core loop to be obvious in seconds.
- **Home cooks.** Making mac and cheese, pizza, fondue or a grilled cheese tonight. They want a blend that works and a plain-language reason why. They aren't chemists.
- **Cheese nerds.** Want the chemistry underneath: compounds, sources, odor maps. Happy to dig, but only after the main result is clear.

The job on the main screen: pick or describe a dish, get a blend, then tweak it by dragging cheeses and sliding shares while you watch how the taste changes.

## Product Purpose

CheeseBlend predicts how any blend of cheeses tastes from published flavour chemistry, then searches 171 cheeses for the blend that best fits a craving. Muse parses the craving, researched the catalogue and explains the result. Success means a judge or cook gets a credible, delicious-sounding blend in under a minute, understands why it works, and enjoys playing with it.

## Brand Personality

Cartoon cheese, but tidy. Playful, warm, confident. The bright yellow, ink outlines and holes are the signature, used with restraint: one clear focal point per area, generous breathing room, few borders. The voice is a friendly cheesemonger, not a lab report: short, concrete and plain, with the numbers available but never shouting.

## Anti-references

- **Cluttered dashboard.** Many boxes, charts and numbers competing at once.
- **Generic AI app.** Sparkle emoji, stock gradients, templated cards that look auto-generated. No emoji in the interface; use custom drawn icons.
- **Kids' game.** So cartoony it stops reading as research-backed.
- **Science paper.** Compound names, cosine and radians front and center. Jargon lives in "Go deeper".

## Design Principles

1. **The loop is the product.** Craving in, blend on the board, taste read-out: all three visible together, and every change answers instantly.
2. **Plain words first, chemistry on request.** Lead with "nutty, melty, 72% match". Compounds, sources and maps sit one level down.
3. **Tidy cartoon.** Personality comes from the illustration, colour and type, not from boxes and decoration. Remove a border before adding one.
4. **Tell people what to do next.** Every result says what is missing and offers a one-tap fix.
5. **Honest predictions.** Always framed as model predictions, with confidence shown quietly, never hidden.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast at least 4.5:1 and at least 3:1 for UI parts. Every action works by tap or click as well as drag. Keyboard focus is visible. Colour is never the only signal (bars carry numbers, gaps carry words). Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
